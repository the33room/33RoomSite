A quirky & eclectic gathering full of books, cocktails, art, local makers, and unexpected treasures.

Step into the season for an afternoon of browsing; cozy charm, playful haunts, and whimsical romance await at Peoria's newest market!

Hosted by nationally acclaimed The 33 Room - serving one-day-only themed signature cocktails - and Kinky Ink, an independent bookstore celebrating romance and women-centered stories.

We’re bringing together a curated lineup of local artists, authors, and entertainers for an adult-focused fall experience with a distinctively magical twist. Come soak in the autumn warmth, witchy aesthetics, and unique romantic flair!
